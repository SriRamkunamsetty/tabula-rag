# HEXFALL — Errata Sheet, First Printing Correction

This errata applies to the Edition 1 rulebook. Where a rule below conflicts
with the base rulebook, this errata is the corrected, official rule.

## Errata 1 — Turn limit (corrects Section 9)

Playtesting after publication showed 60 moves was too short for
experienced players to reach a natural conclusion in a meaningful fraction
of games. The official turn limit is revised upward:

  A game that reaches move 80 without a winner is declared a draw.

## Errata 2 — Surge range (corrects Section 6)

The printed rulebook states a Tower's Surge reaches "up to and including
the third cell." This is correct and is NOT changed by this errata; it is
restated here only because it is the rule most commonly misread during
playtesting. Confirm: the Surge captures enemy Runners in the first, second,
and third cells of the line of sight — a fourth cell is never affected.

No other section of the Edition 1 rulebook is amended by this errata.
